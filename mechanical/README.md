# Mechanical Designs

This section is reserved for Griffen's mechanical design files. No CAD models, fabrication drawings, or mechanical measurements are included yet.

## Where to add files

Create these folders when the corresponding files are available:

| Folder | Intended contents |
| --- | --- |
| `cad/` | Editable parts and assemblies in their original CAD format |
| `drawings/` | Dimensioned drawings and assembly PDFs |
| `exports/` | STEP interchange models and fabrication exports such as STL or DXF |
| `bom/` | Mechanical bill of materials, fasteners, quantities, and materials |
| `images/` | Assembly photos and labelled design views |

Keep editable source files alongside their exported counterparts. Record units, material, revision, and the CAD application/version needed to open each design.

## Design handoff

For each released assembly, include its source model, matching drawings or exports, bill of materials, and assembly notes. Identify mounting interfaces for drive motors, wheels, electronics, and the spinner assembly using verified dimensions.

Mark proposed designs as drafts until fit, fabrication details, and physical testing have been checked.

## Robot and motor references

Griffen is a combat robotics project under development since 2023. The team identifies two wheelchair motors for the mobile base and a starter-motor-driven metal spinner for robot-versus-robot competition.

![Griffen robot](images/metal-monster-robot.jpg)

These images are visual references and do not replace dimensioned drawings or editable CAD.
